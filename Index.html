How the calls work

Every player gets a 0–100 matchup score and a confidence rating. Nothing's hidden — tap a player to see the breakdown.

Hitters: OPS vs the pitcher's hand how hittable the starter is the bullpen behind him ballpark
Pitchers: the offense they face that lineup's strikeout rate park their own form

Confidence reflects how strong the lean is, how much data backs it, and whether the factors agree. If a hitter isn't in the posted lineup, he's flagged and dropped to a sit; if the lineup isn't out yet, confidence is capped.

Tapping a player also shows his last-15-days form and his history against today's pitcher. Coming in the next version: Vegas run totals, advanced Statcast metrics, and an AI write-up for each call.
